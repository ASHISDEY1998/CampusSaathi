The Challenge & The Vision

Why CampusSaathi? Solving Modern Campus Friction

⚠️

Traditional Campus Challenges

  • Information Fragmentation: Master timetables, notices, and exam schedules are scattered across physical boards or irregular WhatsApp groups.
  • Hostel Life Restrictions: Strict regulations (e.g. 2-minute phone windows, Sunday-only 9 AM–5 PM guardian visits, station-leaving passes) cause frequent confusion.
  • Academic Doubt Delays: Students wait days for scheduled doubt-clearing sessions without an instant, verified institutional reference.
  • Security & Role Leakage: Traditional student portals often suffer from role confusion or accidental access between student, teacher, and admin functions.
✨

The CampusSaathi Solution

  • Unified Digital Hub: Centralized dashboard for timetables, attendance, notices, and tickets for +2 students and teachers.
  • 24/7 Context-Grounded AI Companion: An intelligent conversational assistant powered by Google Gemini and trained directly on official PCHSS rules and timetables.
  • Strict Role-Based Security: Complete wall separating Student, Teacher, and Administrator domains with zero cross-contamination.
  • Tesla-Inspired UI: Clean, high-contrast, distraction-free interface with instant mobile responsiveness and dark/light modes.

Artificial Intelligence

How the CampusSaathi RAG Pipeline Operates

Phase A

Offline / Admin Knowledge Ingestion

1. Official Documents

Authentic Markdown records: 22-point student rules, faculty norms, master timetables, academic calendars.

↓
2. Semantic Chunking

Documents split by sections (##, ###) with document IDs, page references, and section titles.

↓
3. Vectorization & Storage

Gemini Embedding generates 768-dimensional dense vectors stored in MongoDB document_chunks.

Phase B

Real-Time Student Query Resolution

1. Student Ask

Student asks: "Can parents visit me on weekdays, and can I keep a mobile phone in the hostel?"

↓
2. Semantic Search

System finds top chunks from general_rules_and_code_of_conduct.md (Rules #13 & #14) with high similarity score.

↓
3. Grounded Answer

Gemini 2.5 Flash generates an instant, 100% factual response: "No, mobile phones are strictly prohibited (Rule 13). Guardians may visit only on Sundays from 9 AM to 5 PM with authority permission (Rule 14)."

System Management

Admin Portal: Complete Control & Safe Cascade Operations

👥

Full Directory CRUD & Account Control

  • Edit User Details Modal (✏️): Update full name, email, department, roll number, academic year, semester, CGPA, phone (for students), or designation & cabin (for teachers).
  • Direct Password Reset (🔑): Update credentials immediately without waiting for old password confirmation.
  • Safe Cascade Deletion (🗑️): Permanently deletes user records from both users and linked students or teachers collections with safety confirmation dialog.
  • Root Admin Protection: Built-in safeguard prevents accidental deletion of the superadmin account.
