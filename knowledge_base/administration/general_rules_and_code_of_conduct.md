# Purnachandra Higher Secondary School, Raghunathpur, Baripada
## General Rules, Code of Conduct & Service Conditions

### Document Metadata
- **Institution:** Purnachandra Higher Secondary School, Raghunathpur, Baripada (PCHSS)
- **Document Title:** Institutional Rules, Student & Guardian Undertaking, and Faculty Service Conditions
- **Effective Academic Session:** 2026–2027
- **Target Audience:** All Students (+2 Science, Commerce, Arts, CBSE), Parents/Guardians, Faculty Members, Hostel Staff, and Administrative Personnel
- **Disciplinary Authorities:** The Principal, Hostel Superintendent, President & Secretary (PCHSS)
- **Administrative Office Hours:** 09:00 AM to 01:30 PM & 03:00 PM to 05:30 PM

## 1. Undertaking of Student & Guardian to Obey the Rules of the Institute

All admitted students and their parents/guardians are legally and academically bound by the following mandatory 22-point institutional undertaking:

### 1. Punctuality & Classroom Entry
The students are strictly required to attend classes according to the scheduled timetable without a moment's delay towards entry into the classroom. Late arrival at classrooms is not permitted.

### 2. Hostel to Classroom Movement
Students departing from the hostels to attend classes must observe total discipline. They must proceed towards the classroom in an orderly single line rather than dispersed groups.

### 3. Classroom to Hostel Return Movement
Upon completion of daily classwork and laboratory sessions, students must return to their respective hostels in the same disciplined manner, walking in a single line.

### 4. Mandatory Prescribed Dress Code
The institute has prescribed a formal dress code / uniform for all admitted students. It must be abided by all students without any dereliction, exception, or violation. Any student violating the dress code will face immediate disciplinary penalties.

### 5. Daily Morning Prayer
Every student must sit for daily prayer at the scheduled time without any delay. Prayer must be offered with a pious mind, complete solemnity, and a sweet, reverent tone.

### 6. Respect for Teachers & Seniors
Every student must maintain utmost regard, courtesy, and respect towards all teachers, faculty members, administrative staff, and seniors within the premises of the institute at all times.

### 7. Classroom Conduct & Sincerity
Every student must remain dedicated, disciplined, and sincere in their studies during class hours without making any noise, commotion, or disturbance.

### 8. Academic Attention & Doubt Clearance
Students must be attentive in all lectures. Every student has full liberty and encouragement to ask questions to the concerned lecturer for their doubt clearance.

### 9. Guided Play & Sports Activities
When students go out for sports or games on the playground, they must be guided and supervised by their respective sports guides / Physical Education teachers.

### 10. Discipline on the Way to Playgrounds
While proceeding to the sports playground, students must walk in an orderly line instead of crowding in haphazard groups.

### 11. Hostel Dining Timings
In the hostel, students must compulsorily take their breakfast, lunch, and dinner strictly within the scheduled dining timings. Missing dining slots or demanding meals outside scheduled hours is prohibited.

### 12. Strict Calling Restrictions & Guardian Phone Call Policy
Due to continuous phone conversations disrupting studies, the management of Purnachandra Higher Secondary School, Raghunathpur enforces strict telephone policies:
- For ordinary check-ins, a student is permitted to converse with their guardian for a **maximum of 2 minutes only**.
- In case of any genuine emergency, the student is allowed to speak with parents/guardians **exclusively in the presence of the Hostel Superintendent or institutional authority**.
- If a student faces any illness or distress, the institutional authority will directly contact the guardians, and remedial measures will be taken jointly as soon as possible.
- All students and guardians must obey this regulation without exception.

### 13. Absolute Ban on Personal Mobile Phones
Under no circumstances whatsoever are students allowed to possess or keep personal mobile phones, smartphones, or unauthorized communication gadgets on campus or in the hostels.

### 14. Guardian Visiting Hours (Sundays Only)
Frequent unscheduled visits by parents disrupt the academic environment. Therefore, guardian visits are restricted to official visiting hours:
- **Visiting Day:** Sundays only
- **Visiting Hours:** 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM
- **Requirement:** Prior permission from the school authority is mandatory before meeting their ward.

### 15. Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy
Ragging in any form within the institution, classrooms, campus grounds, or hostels is strictly prohibited. Any violation will be viewed with extreme severity, resulting in immediate suspension, expulsion, and statutory legal action.

### 16. Hostel Study Hours
Mandatory study hours in the hostel must be maintained sincerely and strictly by all resident students every morning and evening.

### 17. Self-Study Discipline
During designated self-study periods, students must fully engage themselves in their academic curriculum without creating any disturbance, talking, or loitering.

### 18. Evening Return Curfew for Outdoor Activities
Students permitted to go outside the campus for sports, permitted errands, or institutional activities must return to the institute premises before evening without exception.

### 19. Station Leaving Permission Procedure
Students needing to go home must submit a written application to both the **Principal and Hostel Superintendent** for station leaving permission. They may depart only after obtaining formal approved permission and must travel with their parents or authorized guardian.

### 20. Mandatory Parental Escort for Leaving Campus
No student will be granted station leaving permission unless their parents or verified authorized guardians arrive in person to accompany the student.

### 21. Timely Clearance of School Dues
Students and their guardians must clear all school, hostel, tuition, and examination dues in a timely manner prior to appearing in the annual / final board examinations.

### 22. Administrative & School Office Timings
The official institutional office timings are fixed as follows:
- **Morning Session:** 09:00 AM to 01:30 PM
- **Afternoon Session:** 03:00 PM to 05:30 PM
All official payments, applications, queries, and administrative interactions must be conducted within these specified hours.

## 2. Service Conditions & Norms for Faculty Members

Teachers and faculty members recruited to Purnachandra Higher Secondary School, Raghunathpur must abide by the following 12-point service agreement and code of professional conduct:

### 1. Educational Qualifications & Document Verification
All faculty members must possess bonafide, genuine educational qualifications for the post held. If any certificate or credential is found to be non-genuine or forged, the employee's services will be terminated immediately on the date of detection without any prior notice.

### 2. Professional Discipline & Conduct
As faculty members represent the cornerstone of the institution, they must maintain absolute discipline in all respects. If any indiscipline or professional misconduct is observed, the faculty member will receive a formal warning. If behavior is not amended after warnings, their services will be terminated.

### 3. Class Routine Compliance & Doubt-Clearing Duties
Taking classes strictly according to the master routine is the primary and vital duty of every faculty member. Negligence in conducting scheduled classes, designated doubt-clearing sessions, or assigned adjustment/proxy classes will not be tolerated.

### 4. Teaching Excellence & Doubt Resolution
Teachers must deliver each lecture to the highest standards of clarity, satisfaction, and academic excellence. If students ask questions for clarification, the teacher must provide the answer promptly. If the answer is not readily available, the teacher must note the query in their academic diary and clarify it thoroughly in the subsequent doubt-clearing session.

### 5. Punctuality & Casual Leave (C.L.) Forfeiture Rule
Every faculty member must be strictly punctual in arriving at school and signing the attendance register within the prescribed time:
- If a faculty member arrives after the stipulated time, the Principal will mark a **cross mark** in the attendance register.
- If a faculty member arrives late consecutively for three (3) days, **one (1) day of Casual Leave (C.L.) will be forfeited**.

### 6. Working Hours & Leave Sanctioning Policy
- Standard working hours for regular faculties are **7 hours per day**, which may be extended in emergency or institutional circumstances.
- Employees have no inherent right to leave unless it has been officially applied for and formally granted by the authority. The administration reserves the right to disallow leave applications based on academic needs.

### 7. Compulsory Sunday & Holiday Emergency Duty
The school maintains an emergency duty chart for staff attendance on Sundays and holidays. Attendance on designated emergency duty days is compulsory. If an employee fails to attend assigned Sunday/holiday duty without prior approved permission, the employee's salary will be withheld for the unauthorized absence.

### 8. Resignation Policy & Two Months' Notice Requirement
No regular employee may abandon duty without proper notice. Any regular faculty member intending to resign must provide **two (2) months' advance written notice**. If an employee fails to give two months' notice, they must deposit **two (2) months' full salary** with the institution prior to the acceptance of their resignation and official relief from duty.

### 9. Minimum Service Retention Period (2 Years)
The management has established a mandatory **minimum retention period of two (2) years** from the date of joining for all faculty members. This norm applies to all teaching staff.

### 10. Co-Curricular, Extracurricular & Institutional Duties
In addition to classroom instruction, teachers must diligently perform all co-curricular, extracurricular, administrative, and invigilation duties assigned by the President and Principal. Defying administrative directions will result in adverse inferences and disciplinary action.

### 11. Daily Duty Chart Adherence
The school publishes a daily duty chart on the staff notice board covering daily campus monitoring and academic responsibilities. Employees whose names appear on the daily chart must execute their duties meticulously without dereliction.

### 12. Exclusivity of Employment (Prohibition of Dual Engagement)
Faculty members holding full-time agreements with PCHSS are strictly prohibited from engaging in part-time teaching, private coaching centers, or contracts with any other educational institution. Any violation will result in immediate termination of service on the date of detection without prior notice.
