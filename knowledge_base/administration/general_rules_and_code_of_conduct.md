# General Rules, Regulations & Code of Conduct for Students

## Document Metadata
- **Institution:** Purnachandra Group of Institutions
- **Document Title:** Student Handbook: General Rules, Code of Conduct, Attendance & Uniform Regulations
- **Effective Version:** 2024–2026
- **Target Audience:** All Undergraduate and Higher Secondary Students, Parents, Faculty, Administrative Staff
- **Disciplinary Authority:** The Principal of the College

---

## 1. General Rules and Regulations

1.1 **Disciplinary Authority:** The Principal of the College shall be the sole disciplinary authority in respect of all students enrolled in the college.

1.2 **Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy:** Ragging is strictly banned on the college campus. Anyone found guilty of ragging and/or abetting ragging will be punished severely as per the directives of the University Grants Commission (UGC), Government regulations, and the Supreme Court of India. All incidents of ragging will be met with immediate dismissal from the college, and criminal police cases will be registered against offending individuals.

1.3 **Political Activities Prohibition:** In accordance with the institutional policy adopted by the Governing Body and the Parent-Teacher Association (PTA), the College enforces strict regulations on political activities. Students are explicitly forbidden from organizing or participating in any political activity, demonstration, or rally on the campus premises.

1.4 **Mobile Phone & Vehicle Restrictions:** As per Government Order No. `Rt/No.346/05/Higher Edn` dated 01-03-2005:
- Students are **strictly not allowed to use mobile phones** on the college campus.
- Students are permitted to bring two-wheelers only up to the designated parking area allotted to them near the main college gate.
- **Under no circumstances are students allowed to bring four-wheelers (cars) to the college.**

1.5 **College Uniform Mandate:** All students shall compulsorily wear the prescribed college uniform on all working days **except Wednesdays**.
- The uniform symbolizes institutional unity, equity, and identity.
- Parents/guardians must ensure that the uniform and hairstyles of their wards conform to institutional standards of decency, dignity, and modesty.

1.6 **Celebrations & Events Approval:** No celebration, function, or party shall be held on the campus without prior written permission from the Principal.

1.7 **Prohibition of Paid External Entertainment & Fund Raising:**
- Programs organized by external commercial agencies, paid performers, DJs, or commercial musical troupes are strictly prohibited on the campus.
- No form of unauthorized fund raising by students is permitted under any circumstances, preventing extortion or misuse of funds.

1.8 **Vehicles During Celebrations:** No motor vehicles shall be operated or used during any student celebrations inside the college campus or hostel premises.

1.9 **Event Supervision:** All permitted celebrations and cultural programs on campus must be conducted under the direct supervision of the Principal and faculty members deputed by the administration.

1.10 **Campus Entry for Alumni & General Public:** Former students and members of the public are permitted to enter the college campus only for verified, genuine academic or administrative reasons. Former students are advised to carry their punched/expired ID cards for entry. Entry into classrooms without written authorization is strictly barred.

1.11 **Guests & Outsiders:** Students shall not invite any outside guests, students of other institutions, or representatives of external bodies to the campus without explicit prior permission from the Principal.

1.12 **Use of College Insignia & Social Media:** Students shall not use the official college emblem, logo, campus photographs, or institutional symbols on social media platforms (such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, X) or in public programs conducted outside the campus without written consent.

1.13 **Admission Discretion:** The Principal reserves the right to refuse admission or readmission to any candidate without assigning reasons if the authorities determine that admitting the individual would be detrimental to institutional interests or discipline.

1.14 **Punitive Powers:** The Principal is fully empowered to initiate punitive actions against any student for misconduct, indiscipline, or breach of regulations.

1.15 **Acts of Misconduct Defined:** The following specific acts constitute gross misconduct:
- 1.15.1 Staying away from classes or laboratory sessions without sufficient valid reason.
- 1.15.2 Disrespectful or discourteous behavior towards teaching or non-teaching college staff.
- 1.15.3 Instigating student walkouts, strikes, dharnas, or unauthorized gatherings.
- 1.15.4 Loitering in corridors, verandas, or porches during lecture hours. *(During free hours, students are expected to make use of the Central Library).*
- 1.15.5 Unruly behavior or noise causing disturbance to ongoing academic classes.
- 1.15.6 Organizing field trips, tours, or excursions without written sanction from the Principal.
- 1.15.7 Organizing unauthorized meetings, writing on lecture blackboards/whiteboards, or affixing posters on notice boards without permission.
- 1.15.8 Blocking entrance gates, classroom doors, or pedestrian passages, impeding the movement of staff and fellow students.
- 1.15.9 Involvement in physical clashes, brawls, bullying, or harassment of students.
- 1.15.10 Disrupting official functions in the college auditorium by shouting, howling, or disorderly dancing.
- 1.15.11 Resorting to any form of unfair means or malpractice in internal assessments or university examinations.
- 1.15.12 Unauthorized collection of money from students or staff members.
- 1.15.13 Distributing pamphlets, circulars, handbills, or displaying external flags and banners.
- 1.15.14 Scribbling abusive, offensive, or obscene words or graffiti on blackboards, washroom walls, desks, or campus structures.

1.16 **Substance Abuse Ban:** Smoking, consumption of alcoholic beverages, chewing tobacco, and the possession or use of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances are strictly prohibited. Criminal legal proceedings will be initiated immediately against offenders.

1.17 **Vandalism & Damage to Property:** Vandalism and willful destruction of college equipment, laboratory apparatus, or furniture will be punished severely. The full financial cost of replacement and repair will be recovered from the responsible individuals.

1.18 **Campus Cleanliness:** Classrooms and campus grounds must always be kept neat and litter-free. Waste must be disposed of exclusively in designated bins.

1.19 **Valuables & Belongings:** The college administration holds no financial or legal responsibility for the loss of personal money, gadgets, or valuables brought to campus. Students must safeguard their possessions.

1.20 **Consequences of Indiscipline:** Grave insubordination, contempt for authority, willful damage to property, examination malpractice, or moral turpitude constitute grounds for immediate expulsion from the college.

1.21 **Mandatory ID Card Display:** Students must visibly wear their official college ID card tags at all times on the campus. ID cards must be presented on demand to faculty, administrative staff, and security personnel, and are mandatory for all office transactions.

1.22 **Decorum of Dress:** Students must maintain decency, modesty, and academic decorum in their personal attire and general conduct.

1.23 **Punctuality & Morning Prayer:** Students must be seated inside classrooms immediately upon the sounding of the commencement bell. Observance of strict silence in a standing posture is mandatory during the daily morning prayer and National Anthem.

1.24 **Faculty Absence Protocol:** If an instructor is absent, the Class Representative (CR) must immediately contact the Head of the Department (HoD) to arrange alternative academic supervision.

1.25 **Late Entry Protocol:** Latecomers will not be admitted to class without permission from the teacher in charge. No student may leave a class in progress without an approved exit pass.

1.26 **Parent-Teacher Engagement:** Parents and guardians are required to attend scheduled PTA meetings and promptly respond to official communications from college authorities.

1.27 **Staff Authority:** All teaching faculty members hold the responsibility and authority to enforce discipline across classes and general campus areas.

1.28 **College Union Elections:** Elections to the College Union, when conducted, shall strictly adhere to the Presidential mode.

1.29 **Auditorium Gatherings:** When an assembly or special program is scheduled in the auditorium or conference hall, attendance is mandatory; classrooms must be vacated promptly.

1.30 **Off-Campus Conduct:** Students are expected to behave with dignity, courtesy, and integrity outside the campus, recognizing that public behavior directly impacts the institution's reputation.

## 2. Code of Conduct for Students

2.1 **Silence in Academic Corridors:** Any unavoidable movement through verandas, corridors, and adjacent to laboratories or seminar halls during lecture hours must be conducted in silence to prevent disturbance.

2.2 **Campus Curfew Hours:** No student is permitted to remain on the campus after **4:30 PM** unless special written permission has been obtained for authorized institutional activities.

2.3 **Statutory Compliance:** Students must strictly abide by all judicial orders and statutory guidelines issued by the High Court, UGC, and State Government.

2.4 **Utilization of Library During Free Hours:** Students are required to utilize unscheduled or free class periods for quiet study in the Central Library rather than gathering on porches or verandas.

2.5 **Leaving Campus During Working Hours:** No student shall leave the campus during working hours without prior written permission and an approved gate exit pass from the Principal or HoD. Students will not be permitted to leave without verified parental communication or a justifiable emergency.

2.6 **Zones of Strict Silence:** Absolute silence must be observed at all times in the **Central Library**, **College Office**, and **Chapel/Prayer Hall**.

2.7 **Auditorium Decorum:** Dignified behavior is expected during all formal gatherings, guest lectures, and ceremonies.

2.8 **External Competitions:** Prior written sanction from the Principal is mandatory for students representing the college in sports tournaments, cultural fests, or academic competitions off-campus.

2.9 **Conduct Certificates:** A Good Conduct Certificate is an earned institutional endorsement, not a matter of automatic legal right. It is issued by the Principal solely upon the recommendation of the concerned HoD, Mentor, and faculty.

2.10 **Transfer Certificate Authority:** The Principal possesses full authority to issue a Transfer Certificate (TC) to any student whose conduct, attendance, or character is deemed detrimental to institutional harmony.

2.11 **Scale of Disciplinary Penalties:** For acts of indiscipline, the Principal may impose one or more of the following sanctions:
- Monetary fines
- Cancellation of attendance
- Withholding of certificates and grade transcripts
- Suspension or forfeiture of scholarships, concessions, and fee waivers
- Disqualification from participation in sports and arts competitions
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent expulsion

2.12 **Disciplinary Rules Authority:** The Principal and the College Disciplinary Committee may frame and enforce standing or temporary regulations governing conduct across campus and affiliated hostels.

2.13 **Property Protection Legal Action:** Any person responsible for damaging institutional infrastructure will face legal prosecution under the **Prevention of Damage to Public Property Act 1984**, alongside full monetary recovery.

2.14 **Suspended Students:** Any student placed under disciplinary suspension is legally debarred from entering the college campus without formal written permission.

2.15 **Off-Campus Agitations:** Participating in strikes or agitations at other collegiate institutions is treated as a grave disciplinary breach.

2.16 **Outsider Meeting Regulations:** Outsiders wishing to meet faculty or the Principal must register at the security gate with government-issued photo ID. Direct interactions between unauthorized outsiders and students within campus boundaries are forbidden.

2.17 **Disruption of Administration:** Any attempt by outside individuals or students to disrupt administrative functioning, examination cells, or office operations will be treated as unlawful criminal trespass. Legal action, including contempt of court, will be pursued.

2.18 **Police & Media Protocol:** Outsiders, media representatives, and law enforcement personnel shall not enter campus premises without notifying and securing permission from the Principal.

2.19 **Audio/Video Recording Ban:** Taking photographs, recording videos, or publishing audio-visual material captured on campus without written authorization is strictly prohibited.

2.20 **Official Media Coverage:** Programs conducted within the college may only be photographed or recorded by officially designated personnel.

2.21 **Vehicle Registration Mandate:** All students and staff using two-wheelers must register their vehicle registration number and valid driving license with campus security. Specially-abled or medically certified students may apply for special permits to park adjacent to academic buildings.

2.22 **Gate Inspection:** Security personnel are authorized to inspect vehicles, verify identity cards, and record entry/exit timestamps in the gate register.

2.23 **Grievance Redressal:** Student grievances or complaints must be submitted through formal channels via Mentors, HoDs, or the institutional Grievance Redressal Cell.

2.24 **Right to Amend:** The Governing Body reserves the right to amend or update institutional regulations as academic and statutory requirements evolve.

2.25 **Obligation to Read Notices:** Ignorance of published notices, academic circulars, or timetable revisions posted on official boards and portals cannot be cited as an excuse for non-compliance.

2.26 **Ultimate Authority:** The Principal remains the final authority regarding the interpretation and enforcement of all institutional rules and regulations.

## 3. Attendance & Leave of Absence

3.1 **Working Hours & Calculation of Absence:**
- College working hours: **9:30 AM to 4:30 PM**.
- All working days are calculated as full working days.
- **Absence during even one hour in either the forenoon or afternoon session will be counted as half a day of absence.**
- Latecomers will not be awarded attendance for the session.

3.2 **Mandatory 75% Attendance Requirement:**
- A Semester Certificate of Attendance and Progress (prerequisite for university examination registration and semester progression) will **not** be granted unless the student has attended **at least 75% of total working days**.
- The Principal must also be satisfied with the student's academic progress and conduct.

3.3 **Leave of Absence Procedures:**
- Short leave (1 to 2 days) may be granted by the concerned HoD or Academic Mentor.
- Applications for extended leave must be addressed directly to the Principal.
- **Medical Sick Leave:** Any sick leave exceeding **five (5) consecutive working days** must be supported by a registered medical practitioner's certificate. The medical certificate must be submitted immediately upon rejoining.

3.4 **Unexcused Absence & Exit Passes:** Absence without approved leave is punishable. Students requiring early departure must obtain an official written exit pass from their HoD.

3.5 **Consecutive Absence Penalty (10 Days):**
- **Unexcused absence for ten (10) consecutive working days will result in the immediate removal of the student's name from the college rolls.**
- Readmission can only be granted at the Principal's discretion upon payment of prescribed re-enrollment fees.

3.6 **Duty Leave Policy:** Duty leave for students representing the college or university in inter-collegiate sports, cultural competitions, NSS, or NCC will only be granted upon advance submission of an official application endorsed by the faculty coordinator.

## 4. Identity Cards & Uniform Policy

4.1 **Identity Card Regulations:**
- Official student ID cards must be worn visibly on institutional lanyards at all times on campus.
- The ID card is essential for:
  - Transit bus concessions
  - Tuition and examination fee payments
  - Central Library entry and book circulation
  - Casting votes in student union elections
  - Caution deposit refunds
  - Issue of bonafide, character, and transfer certificates
- Students found on campus without valid ID cards are subject to fines or dismissal from campus premises.
- **Duplicate ID Card Fee:** A replacement card will be issued only under verified circumstances upon payment of a **₹150 penalty fee**.

4.2 **College Uniform Schedule:**
- **Compulsory Uniform Days:** Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday.
- **Uniform Exemption Day:** **Wednesdays only** (students may wear modest casual attire conforming to dignity standards).
- ID card must be formally surrendered to the college office at the time of issuing the Transfer Certificate (TC).
